Background

I am a J.D. candidate at UC Davis School of Law, set to graduate in May 2029, and I plan to build my career in prosecution. I am from Davis, California, and I am completing my legal education here.

Before law school, I earned a B.S. in Business Administration (Finance) with a minor in Philosophy from California State University, Sacramento, in December 2025. I began my studies at American River College, where I earned an A.S. in Business Administration with President’s Highest Honors. I maintained a 4.0 GPA at both schools.

Legal and Professional Experience

In 2025, I interned at California Probate & Trust Law Office in Sacramento. There I wrote and filed petitions with courts in several counties, which gave me early, hands-on experience with the mechanics of court filings. I also digitized and organized client files and resolved technical issues to keep the firm running smoothly.

From 2023 to 2026, I worked as the office administrator at Irish Iron in Carmichael. I improved internal payment tracking by implementing CRM and spreadsheet software, and I built a system to track accounts totaling more than $1,000,000 in annual revenue.

Service and Leadership

As an undergraduate, I founded and served as president of Gamers Gift at Sacramento State, a campus club based on a nonprofit of the same name. The club uses video games and other electronic media to help ease the stress of prolonged hospital stays, loneliness, poverty, and other difficult circumstances.

I wrote and refined the club’s constitution and governing documents and completed the required training so the club complied with all campus policies and procedures. I also built relationships with administrators at children’s hospitals, senior centers, and retirement homes so we could deliver games on a consistent schedule.

Where I’m Headed

My goal is a career as a prosecutor. I am early in my legal education and glad to hear from practicing attorneys, professors, and fellow students about the work and the path to it.
